# Submission-day runbook

This checklist is for the author-controlled ARR/NAACL path. It does not authorize new provider calls or changes to the frozen evidence set.

## Before opening OpenReview

- [ ] Decide whether an ICLR 2027 upload already exists; do not submit a late ICLR upload.
- [ ] If ARR is active, confirm the October 12, 2026 submission deadline and the NAACL-primary/COLING-secondary routing in the live OpenReview form.
- [ ] Run the local final handoff gate and confirm `passed`, 10/10 checks, and zero provider calls.
- [ ] Confirm the anonymous source archive is the current 10-entry archive and that its hash matches the author checklist.
- [ ] Keep author names, ORCID, affiliations, conflicts, and emails out of the anonymous archive.

## OpenReview author and service gates

- [ ] Every author has a complete OpenReview profile and ORCID.
- [ ] Every author completes reviewer registration by the applicable deadline.
- [ ] Designate one qualified ARR service contributor; record the qualification route and ensure the contributor supports at most two submissions.
- [ ] Plan to complete the contributor form within 48 hours after the submission deadline.
- [ ] Confirm that the author list will not change at commitment.

## Upload decision

- [ ] Upload the manuscript-only package unless TacoMAS redistribution/license permission is confirmed.
- [ ] Do not upload raw provider traces, raw canaries, local audit logs, credentials, or configuration files.
- [ ] Save the OpenReview submission ID, exact uploaded PDF/source hashes, and timestamp in the identity-bearing author record.

## After upload

- [ ] Re-run the local handoff gate only if the uploaded files differ from the verified package.
- [ ] Do not start a new provider experiment or revise a headline claim during the review window without a new preregistered question and an explicit amendment.
